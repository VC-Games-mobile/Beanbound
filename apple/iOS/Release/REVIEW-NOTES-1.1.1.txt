Beanbound 1.1.1 (10) imports the upstream settings update. New independent music and sound-effect volume sliders, reduced motion, gameplay hints, auto-pause preferences, and reset-settings controls. Settings persist locally.

For App Review: the optional speed-boost Easter egg is activated by tapping Haptic feedback ten times within four seconds in Settings. It doubles simulation speed for five minutes and restores the original haptics preference after activation. The pause-menu Restart button cancels it. No purchase or login is required. The Moon feature remains disabled.

No accounts, ads, purchases, external services, or in-app analytics SDKs. All game assets are bundled locally and the game works offline. No regional feature differences.

Build 8 adds event-specific native iPhone haptics: gentle rate-limited pickups, medium shield impacts, success notifications for evolutions/milestones, and a single heavy game-over impact. The Haptic feedback switch gates every cue.

Build 9 adds subtle environmental haptics: soft soil bumps at most every 900 ms, light rain taps at most every 450 ms while water is being restored, and a native priority delay following hits, milestones, or game over. The same Haptic feedback switch controls these effects.

Build 10 adds an optional, very low-intensity continuous Core Haptics texture while growing in dirt or rain, beneath the existing transient cues. It stops on pause, menu, game over, background, disabled haptics, or loss of gameplay heartbeat; priority cues temporarily duck it. Unsupported hardware produces no rumble.
