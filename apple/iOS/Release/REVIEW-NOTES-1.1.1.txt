Beanbound 1.1.1 (12) adds independent music and sound-effect volume levels, reduced motion, gameplay-hint and auto-pause preferences, reset settings, and improved native iPhone haptics. Back is the first Settings control. Preferences and progress are saved locally.

To review: tap Play for the normal 100,000-metre climb, or Endless Mode for continuing runs. Drag left/right to steer, catch water, and avoid hazards. Pause opens the pause menu. Settings & More opens the new preferences. Enable Haptic feedback on a supported iPhone to feel pickups, shield hits, evolutions/milestones, game over, and continuous dirt/rain texture. The texture stops on pause, background, menu, game over, or disabling haptics; major events take priority. Unsupported hardware, including iPads without haptic hardware, has no rumble.

Easter egg disclosure: tap Haptic feedback ten times within four seconds in Settings to activate double simulation speed for five minutes. The original haptics preference is restored after activation. The pause-menu Restart button cancels the boost. No purchase or login is required. The Moon feature remains disabled.

Purpose and audience: a casual skill-based arcade game providing offline entertainment through short runs, growing a beanstalk from underground to space and unlocking evolutions.

No setup, login credentials, accounts, sample files, ads, purchases, user-generated content, external services, or in-app analytics SDKs are required. All game code, artwork, fonts, and music are bundled locally. The developer has permission to distribute the assets, with font licenses in Credits. No regulated services. Features are consistent across regions; the game is in English.

Validation: iPhone/iPad WKWebView integration tests passed for touch, fonts, audio, lifecycle, persistence, Endless mode, and new settings. Engine tests cover twelve Endless milestones, normal victory, independent volume controls, secret activation/expiry, haptic routing and off/pause stops. Build 12 was exported from the release archive with development signing and installed/launched on a physical iPhone 14. The owner tested and accepted the latest haptic and Settings changes. No physical iPad installation was requested for this release.
