How to play

Drag anywhere to steer your shoot. Catch raindrops before you run dry, avoid hazards, and climb to 100,000 m to reach space.

Obstacles

  • Rocks: found underground
  • Birds: fly around the ground layer
  • Clouds, storms, and balloons: hazards in the sky

Abilities

  • Water:  restores your water
  • Sun Orb:  gives you a shield and bonus score
  • Seed Pack:  extremely rare; gives you one random ability
Possible abilities
  • Dehydration:  water drains faster for a short time
  • Big:  makes your shoot larger
  • Small:  makes your shoot smaller
  • Slow:  slows your climb
  • Fast:  speeds up your climb

Dehydration is the most common. The others are less common.

Endless Mode is coming soon. It isn't finished yet, but it'll be added in a future update.
