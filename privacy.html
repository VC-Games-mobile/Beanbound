Beanbound privacy policy

Effective October 3, 2026. This policy covers the Beanbound iPhone and iPad app published by Brent Hassevoort, with VC Games branding.

Your game stays on your device

Beanbound does not collect or send personal information to us. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases. The game works offline.

High scores, unlocked evolutions, and preferences are saved locally on your device. They are not sent to a Beanbound server or synchronized between devices. Your device’s own backup settings may include this local data.

Sharing and support

If you choose to share a score, iOS opens its share sheet. The app or service you choose handles the information you share under its own privacy policy.

Privacy and support links open external websites. Our support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which handles website requests under GitHub’s privacy statement. Visiting those pages is optional.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, use our support issue tracker. Issues are public, so please do not include private information. Any policy changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
