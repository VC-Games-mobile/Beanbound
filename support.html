Beanbound support

Grow a beanstalk from deep underground all the way to space.

Playing on iPhone and iPad

Tap Play, then drag your finger left and right to steer. Collect rain and seeds, avoid hazards, and discover new evolutions as you climb. Use the pause button to take a break. The game supports portrait and landscape orientation and requires iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.

Sound and saved progress

Music, sound, haptics, and other preferences are available in Settings. Check your device volume if music is quiet. High scores and evolutions are stored on this device; they do not synchronize across devices. Reset settings asks for confirmation.

Beanbound is free, works offline, and has no ads or purchases.

Report a problem

Open a support issue on GitHub with your device model, iOS version, Beanbound version, and what happened. Please avoid posting private information. A GitHub account is needed to submit an issue.
